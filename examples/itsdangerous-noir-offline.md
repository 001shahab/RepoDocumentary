## Act II: The Lead

From 2018-10-26 to 2021-02-09, 169 commits arrived, led by dependabot-preview[bot], Contributor R and Contributor A. Most of the action happened in the project root, src/ and requirements/, with 2372 lines written and 1262 taken away.

The record from this chapter reads: "Updated changelog and docs", "drop support for Python 2 and 3.5", "create a new doc for general concepts", "add typing with mypy".

## Act III: The Double-Cross

From 2021-02-15 to 2022-11-01, 169 commits arrived, led by Contributor R, dependabot[bot] and pre-commit-ci[bot]. Most of the action happened in requirements/, src/ and the project root, with 657 lines written and 1124 taken away.

## Act IV: The Long Night

From 2022-11-07 to 2025-06-14, 169 commits arrived, led by Contributor R, dependabot[bot] and pre-commit-ci[bot]. Most of the action happened in the project root, requirements/ and .github/, with 4067 lines written and 2939 taken away.

The record from this chapter reads: "[pre-commit.ci] pre-commit autoupdate (#320)", "update project files", "improve typing", "use uv".

The biggest single change. "use uv" touched 21 files, adding 2198 and removing 440 lines.

The great purge. "drop end of life python versions" deleted 902 lines while adding only 96.

## Epilogue

Over 13 years, 11 months, 44 contributors made 677 commits, writing 15277 lines and deleting 10892. The project speaks mostly Python, Markdown and HTML. Its favourite words: "bump", "pre-commit", "autoupdate", "actions", "version".

I closed the terminal. Somewhere out there, the build was still running.
